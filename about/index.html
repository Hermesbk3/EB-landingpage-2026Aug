About ecomblade

Research tools for sellers who need clean signals.

ecomblade helps operators compare demand, pricing, revenue, and product movement across modern marketplaces without turning research into spreadsheet archaeology.

Why we exist

Better product decisions should not require five tabs and a guess.

Sellers are expected to make fast inventory, pricing, and launch decisions while the market keeps moving. ecomblade brings the key numbers into one focused workspace so research feels direct, comparable, and practical.

The product is built for Shopee, TikTok Shop, Lazada, Amazon, Temu, and cross-marketplace sellers who care less about dashboards for their own sake and more about choosing what to test, scale, or ignore.
