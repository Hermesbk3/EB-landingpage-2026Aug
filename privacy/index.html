Privacy Policy

How ecomblade handles your information.

This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have when using the ecomblade website and platform.

Last updated August 4, 2026

1. Information we collect

We may collect account information such as your name, email address, login details, billing-related information, and messages you send to support. We also collect usage information about how the website and platform are accessed and used.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, secure, and improve ecomblade.
  • Manage accounts, subscriptions, billing, and support.
  • Understand product usage and improve marketplace research workflows.
  • Send service-related messages and important account notices.

3. Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies, pixels, and analytics technologies to operate the website, understand performance, measure campaigns, and improve the user experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

4. Service providers

We may share information with trusted service providers that help us operate ecomblade, such as hosting, payment processing, analytics, customer support, and security tools. These providers are only permitted to use information as needed to provide services to us.

5. Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal or accounting requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and how it is used.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee perfect security, so you should use strong passwords and protect your account access.

7. Your choices

You may request access, correction, deletion, or export of your personal information where applicable. Some requests may be limited by legal, security, or operational requirements.

8. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@ecomblade.com.
