FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where does your data come from?

We aggregate publicly available marketplace data and process it through our own pipeline to surface sales estimates, revenue, pricing, and reviews. The data is continuously updated to reflect current market conditions.

How is this different from other research tools?

Most tools overwhelm you with dozens of features across a single marketplace. Ecomblade pulls data from multiple global marketplaces into one clean view — only the metrics that matter, with zero noise. Less tabs, less guessing, faster decisions.

Do I need to integrate my seller account?

No. Ecomblade is product research — you're looking at the market, not your own listings. No account connection required, ever. Sign up with email and you're using the product in under 60 seconds.

Is there a free trial?

Ecomblade is free to start — no credit card, no time limit. Use it for as long as you want. When you need full access across all marketplaces, Pro is $19/month.

How quickly can I get started?

Sign up with email and phone. That's it. No onboarding calls, no setup wizard, no waiting for approval. You're researching products within 60 seconds.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. One click from your dashboard. No retention flows, no calls, no guilt. If you're not getting value, we'd rather you leave easily than stay frustrated.
